# Canonical fallback license text

`MIT-SPDX.txt` is the unmodified canonical MIT text from SPDX license-list-data
v3.27.0: https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/blob/v3.27.0/text/MIT.txt.

The exact old `block` 0.1.6 , `malloc_buf` 0.0.6 and `dispatch` 0.2.0 releases declare MIT in their
original Cargo manifests but omit a license file even at their release commits.
Their notices retain that declaration, original authors, registry archive SHA256
and exact source commit, plus this canonical text. Its copyright placeholders
remain template placeholders; no year or copyright holder is invented. Other
missing licenses still fail notice generation. Actual supplied notices take
precedence and are retained without alteration.
